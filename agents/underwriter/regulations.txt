CHAPTER B3-3: INCOME ASSESSMENT
===============================

REGULATION B3-3.1-01: General Income Information
------------------------------------------------
The lender must verify the borrower's employment for the most recent two years.
- Variable income (bonuses, commissions, overtime) requires a 2-year history to be considered stable.
- If less than 2 years but more than 1 year, may be used with strong compensating factors.
- Gaps in employment > 1 month must be explained.

REGULATION B3-3.1-08: Rental Income
-----------------------------------
- Rental income from the subject property (2-4 units) can be used to qualify.
- Must use 75% of the gross lease amount to account for vacancy.
- If the property is the borrower's primary residence, rental income from the main unit (boarder income) is generally NOT acceptable unless it is a generic ADU with separate independent access.

REGULATION B3-6-02: Debt-to-Income Ratios
-----------------------------------------
- Maximum DTI is generally 45% for manually underwritten loans.
- Exceptions up to 50% allowed with strong compensating factors (e.g., 12 months reserves, LTV < 60%).
- Student loans: Use 1% of outstanding balance or the actual amortized payment, whichever is greater, if on IBR plan (unless $0).

CHAPTER B4: PROPERTY APPRAISAL
==============================

REGULATION B4-1.3-05: Property Condition
-----------------------------------------
- The property must be safe, sound, and structurally secure.
- "C5" and "C6" condition ratings are ineligible for delivery.
- Significant deferred maintenance (e.g., leaky roof, foundation cracks) must be repaired prior to close.
- Minor cosmetic issues (worn carpet, old paint) are acceptable (C4 condition).

REGULATION B4-1.4-01: Appraisal Report
--------------------------------------
- Appraisals must be based on at least 3 comparable sales closed within 12 months.
- Value adjustments must be bracketed.

CHAPTER B5: SPECIAL PRODUCTS
============================

REGULATION B5-6-03: HomeReady Mortgage
--------------------------------------
- Max LTV 97%.
- Borrower income must be <= 80% AMI (Area Median Income).
- Reduced MI coverage allowed.
